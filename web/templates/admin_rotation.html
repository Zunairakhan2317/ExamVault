Key & share rotation

Rotation replaces the key shares of an exam that is not released yet. You open a ceremony; then k current holders approve it, each with their own login and private key. When enough have approved, the server rebuilds the old key in memory only, re-encrypts the paper under a brand-new key, issues new shares to the new holder set and retires the old ones. Revoked holders cannot approve and hold nothing afterwards.
